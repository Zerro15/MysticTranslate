The upstream NOTICE describes the broader Screen Translator APK. The MysticTranslate
APK includes AndroidX / Compose, Kotlin and coroutines, OkHttp / Okio, and Google
ML Kit Chinese Text Recognition and Translation with their transitive dependencies.
It does not include the upstream native LLM, speech stack or model assets.
Google ML Kit is governed by its own terms: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
Images and text used by on-device ML Kit are processed locally. As documented by
Google, the SDK may still download models and collect performance or usage metrics.

The Umi-OCR protocol adapter requires a separately hosted service; no Umi-OCR server
is distributed in this APK. No OpenAI-compatible provider credentials are bundled.
